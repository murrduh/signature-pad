AGD Signature Log - setup and use

First time (needs internet once):
1. On the tablet, open the link you were given in Chrome.
2. Tap the Chrome menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen", then "Install".
3. Open "AGD Signature Log" from the home screen. It now works with no internet.

Signing:
1. Tap "Open log" and pick the maintenance log (.xlsx) from Downloads, Drive or OneDrive.
2. Find the row. Tap "Technician signs" or "Staff signs".
3. Check the printed name, sign in the white area, then tap Accept.
4. Tap "Save workbook". Use Share to send it to Drive, OneDrive or email, or Download.
   Your original file is never changed.

Print or PDF: tap "Print view", then "Print / Save as PDF".

If the app closes before you save, reopen it. Your signatures are kept.
